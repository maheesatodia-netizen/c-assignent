➤ HTTPS - hypertext transfer protocol secure - used for safely transforming information Between a website and the browser.

  ✦2. What is the difference between IPv4 and IPv6? Answer in 2 lines :

➤ IPv4 uses short, number-only addresses and is running out of unique space for new devices.
➤ IPv6 uses long, combination addresses with numbers and letters so billions of new devices can connect to the internet.

  ✦4. What do these HTTP status codes mean: 200, 301, 404, 500, 403 ?  :

➤ 200 – The request was successful.
➤ 301 – The page has been moved to another address.
➤ 404 – The page could not be found.
➤ 500 – There is a problem on the server.
➤ 403 – You do not have permission to access the page.

➤ TLD: The top-level domain.

➤ Domain: The main registered domain name.

➤ Subdomain: A hostname under google.com.
